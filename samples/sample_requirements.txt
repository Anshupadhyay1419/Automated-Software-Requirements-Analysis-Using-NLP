ShopEasy Online Store - Client Brief

Hi team, thanks for meeting with us last week. Our company was founded in 2009 and we sell home furniture across three cities.

Customers should register, log in, search products, and place orders. Customers must also be able to track their deliveries.

Guests can browse the catalogue but must not place orders. Administrators can add products and update prices. They can also export sales reports to PDF.

If a payment fails, the system shall notify the customer and cancel the order. Only managers can approve refunds above 500 dollars.

The website should be very fast. Product search results must appear within 2 seconds. The system must support 5000 concurrent users.

All passwords must be stored in encrypted form. The system must never store card numbers in plain text.

The checkout page should be easy to use. It should also look modern.

The website must be available 99.9% of the time. Reports are generated every week.

Shoppers must be able to sign in to the website. Customers can leave a review after a delivery.

Customers can cancel an order at any time. Customers must not cancel an order after it has been shipped.

Search results must appear within 5 seconds.

The system should handle returns, exchanges, etc.
